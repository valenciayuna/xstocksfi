01 — xStocks

Stocks, but composable onchain.

xStocks are tokenized representations of traditional stocks and ETFs. The important shift is not simply putting a stock into a wallet—it is making that asset transferable and usable inside crypto-native infrastructure, subject to the applicable product and jurisdictional restrictions.

Tokenized exposure

Traditional financial assets can be represented as blockchain tokens backed by the corresponding underlying assets.

ONCHAIN

Onchain

Users can interact with tokenized assets through blockchain wallets and decentralized infrastructure.

COMPOSE

Composable

The interesting part: tokenized assets can potentially become building blocks inside other onchain markets.

02 — Custom Pairs

The pair is the story.

Historically, a crypto token might launch against an asset such as SOL. Custom Pairs changes the design space by allowing supported assets to be used as the other side of a market.

Traditional crypto market
MEME ↔ SOL

The meme token's market is priced against a crypto-native asset.

Custom Pair concept

The interesting question isn't “Can stocks go onchain?”

04 — The bigger picture

Is the launchpad becoming a market primitive?

Custom Pairs point toward a broader idea: instead of every new token being denominated against the same few crypto assets, onchain markets can potentially be built around a much wider universe of liquid assets.

MEME → SOL is one market.
MEME → TSLAx is another.
MEME → SPYx is another.

The pair itself becomes part of the product.
05 — Don't ignore the risks

New primitives come with new failure modes.

This is an educational overview, not financial advice. Tokenized equities are not automatically identical to holding the underlying security through a traditional brokerage.

Liquidity

Thin liquidity can create large price impact and make markets difficult to enter or exit.

Pricing

Onchain markets and traditional markets may operate on different schedules and reference different liquidity venues.

Regulation

Availability, transferability and access can depend on jurisdiction, product structure and applicable restrictions.

Always verify the current product documentation, supported assets, jurisdictions, backing model and exact market mechanics before treating a tokenized asset as equivalent to the underlying security.
